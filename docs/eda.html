Distributions

All three are right-skewed: most bookings are short, made a few weeks ahead and around €100 per night. The dashed line is the median.

Cancellation by attribute

Each bar is the share of canceled bookings in that group; the dashed line is the overall rate (27.7%). Large gaps mean the attribute is useful for classification.

Correlation with is_canceled

Pearson correlation of numeric attributes with the class label. All values are weak (|r| < 0.2): no single attribute decides cancellation, so models must combine several.

Implications for modelling

01

Lead time first

Cancellation rises from 8.5% to 41% with lead time — expected to be the first split of the decision tree.

02

Combinations matter

Weak linear correlations but large group differences point to tree-based models over linear ones.

03

Imbalance

27.7% positive class: report precision, recall and F1, not only accuracy.
