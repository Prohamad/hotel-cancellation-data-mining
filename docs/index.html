The project

Problem and purpose

What we investigate, why it matters and who benefits.

Problem

Cancellations cost hotels revenue

Hotels need to identify the factors associated with booking cancellations to better manage capacity and reduce revenue loss.

Purpose

Predict cancellation at booking time

Analyse hotel booking data and predict whether a booking will be canceled using classification techniques, using only what is known when the booking is made.

Who benefits

Hotels, agencies, researchers

Hotel managers (overbooking, staffing, deposit policy), online travel agencies (booking quality) and researchers studying cancellation behaviour.

Research questions

Three questions

Each question uses a different data mining task from the course.

RQ1classification
Can booking cancellation be predicted from attributes known at booking time, and which classifier performs best?
Classification
RQ2numeric prediction
Does the average daily rate (ADR) vary by arrival month and hotel type, reflecting seasonal demand?
Numeric prediction
RQ3clustering
Can bookings be grouped into distinct segments, and do these segments differ in cancellation?
Clustering
